Operations Layer

One console for the work around Casa.

The Management Console manages organisations, gangs, transport reachability, backups, and support access without becoming the automation authority.

Product Model

Management does not need a central Casa cloud.

The console coordinates management through Casa transports. It does not become the automation authority. Gangs define the shared context; the management layer makes them easier to operate.

1Casa

Runs automation locally.

2Bearer transports

LAN, Pusher, whrelay, webhooks.

3Management Console

Observes, configures, and recovers through transports.
